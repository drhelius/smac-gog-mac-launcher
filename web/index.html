Centauri

Bring your GOG copy of Alpha Centauri and Alien Crossfire to your Mac.

A native launcher that imports your game, keeps your saves on your Mac, and lets you choose fullscreen or a window.

Development preview
A public download is being prepared. Compatibility testing is still in progress.
View the project on GitHub

Your game, your saves

Requires your own GOG Planetary Pack. Game files are not included. The original installation is preserved, and after initial setup you can play offline.

The first tested system is an Apple M4 running macOS 26.6.2. Rosetta is required on Apple Silicon. See the project's validation notes for supported features and remaining checks.
